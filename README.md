KaamAI — AI-Powered Business Assistant for Street Vendors

📌 Overview

KaamAI is a React-based AI business assistant designed specifically for Indian street-food vendors and micro-entrepreneurs.

The application provides a simple mobile-first dashboard that helps vendors manage:

* 📊 Daily sales and demand forecasting
* 📦 Inventory and restocking
* 💰 Revenue, expenses, and profit
* 🏛️ Government schemes and financial support
* 🤖 AI-powered business assistance

The prototype demonstrates how AI and data-driven insights can help small businesses make better day-to-day decisions.

⸻

✨ Features

1. 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of the vendor’s business performance.

Features include:

* Today’s revenue
* Number of orders
* Average order value
* Tomorrow’s AI demand forecast
* Product-level demand percentages
* Weekly sales visualization
* AI-generated business insights

Demand predictions consider factors such as:

* Day of the week
* Weather
* Nearby events
* Historical sales
* Exam schedules

⸻

2. 📦 Inventory Management

The Inventory section helps vendors monitor their available stock.

It provides:

* Current inventory levels
* Minimum safe-stock levels
* Automatic stock status
* Critical/Low/OK indicators
* Recommended restocking
* Estimated shopping expenses
* Suggested stores for purchasing supplies

Example:

Paneer is below the safe-stock threshold, so the system recommends purchasing additional paneer before the next business day.

⸻

3. 💰 Finance

The Finance section provides a simplified financial dashboard.

It tracks:

* Monthly revenue
* Monthly expenses
* Net profit
* Profit margin
* Daily average revenue
* Recent transactions
* Cash and UPI payments
* Expense breakdown

The expense breakdown categorizes spending into:

* Raw materials
* Gas & fuel
* Transport
* Other expenses

⸻

4. 🏛️ Government Schemes

KaamAI highlights government schemes that may be useful for street vendors and micro-entrepreneurs.

The prototype includes:

* PM SVANidhi
* PM Vishwakarma
* PMEGP

Each scheme contains:

* Scheme description
* Eligibility/status indicator
* Application call-to-action
* Basic application guidance

⸻

5. 🤖 Ask AI

The Ask AI section provides a conversational business assistant.

Users can ask questions such as:

* “Kal kitna stock karun?”
* “PM SVANidhi kaise milega?”
* “Price kaise badhaaun?”
* “Exam season mein kya karen?”

The assistant is designed to respond in a practical Hinglish style.

The prototype uses the Anthropic Messages API for generating AI responses.
